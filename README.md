## Where to start

Three editorial picks for someone who has never made a series with AI. They are an editor's choice, not a ranking; everything below is listed alphabetically.

1. [SceneMixer](https://scenemixer.com/) — Script or novel in, multi-episode series out: the text is parsed into cast, locations, props and episodes, every character gets a reference sheet, every episode a storyboard, every segment a video clip (Wan 3.0 by default, other tiers selectable), and the clips are assembled into downloadable episodes. Dialogue is spoken in 17 language options; 16:9 by default with 9:16 selectable. 23 public sample series and per-step pricing are on the site.
2. [Showrunner (Fable Studio)](https://www.showrunnerstudio.com/) — Serialized AI video app where users build a show world with persistent characters and generate, watch and remix micro-drama episodes.
3. [LTX Studio](https://ltx.io/studio) — Browser-based AI production suite from Lightricks covering script-to-video, AI storyboards, pitch decks and reusable 'Elements', running LTX models plus partner models.

## Script or novel to series

Input: a script, a novel or a story idea. Output: episodes with a recurring cast.

- [AI Drama Generator (aidramagenerator.ai)](https://www.aidramagenerator.ai/) — Web tool that structures story ideas, scripts or novel chapters into serialized short-drama episodes with voiceover, lip sync and subtitles.
- [AI Dramas (aidramas.com)](https://aidramas.com/) — Script-style editor that renders cast-referenced 4-15 second vertical clips and chains them into short-drama episodes with lip-synced dialogue.
- [Anijam](https://www.anijam.ai/tools/ai-series-generator/) — AI series generator with a chat agent that outlines seasons, locks characters and renders lip-synced multi-scene episodes; also on iOS.
- [AnimateAI](https://animateai.pro/) — AI animation video generator for animated series that creates reusable characters, editable storyboards with narration, and full videos from a story idea.
- [AniShort (global)](https://global.anishort.ai/) — Node-based infinite-canvas collaboration platform for AI film and TV drama production with storyboard, 3D director, review and team features.
- [Arcloop](https://arcloop.ai/) — AI storytelling engine that turns a premise or script into vertical drama and anime episodes with consistent characters, voices and subtitles.
- [AutoWeeb](https://autoweeb.com/) — AI anime production tool for designing character sheets, writing scene-by-scene storyboards and animating shots with Seedance into anime shorts, episodes and series.
- [character.app](https://www.character.app/) — Show-making app that builds a world and cast once and generates episodes in 2D, 3D or live-action styles with a Director agent.
- [Crano AI](https://crano.ai/ai-short-drama-generator) — AI image and video site with a short drama workflow for cast images, scene setup, clips and voiceover, aimed at recurring episodes.
- [DirectStudio AI](https://directstudio.ai/) — Chat-driven video agent that writes a script, generates storyboards and renders micro dramas, short films and ads with consistent cast and locations.
- [DomoAI (AI Drama Generator)](https://www.domoai.app/en/scenarios/ai-drama-generator) — AI video app with a drama scenario that builds short vertical drama beats from per-role reference images and named-speaker dialogue prompts.
- [DramaSo](https://drama.so/) — Vertical short drama maker that maps viral clips into beat sheets, previews dialogue audio-first and renders 9:16 episodes with a character lock.
- [Dramily](https://dramily.com/) — Short-drama production workspace for scripts, reusable assets, reviewed storyboards and Seedance video generation, supporting up to 120 episodes per project.
- [Dreamina AI Short Drama (CapCut)](https://dreamina.capcut.com/ai-video/ai-short-drama) — Dreamina's short drama page for generating drama clips from prompts with Multiframes (up to 10 images) for continuity and built-in lip-synced audio.
- [GenCartoon](https://gencartoon.com/) — Prompt-to-cartoon video generator that keeps the same characters across episodes for cartoon series.
- [ImagineArt AI Drama Studio](https://www.imagine.art/ai-drama-studio) — Feature inside the ImagineArt creative suite for writing, casting and producing microdrama episodes with saved characters, voices and 9:16 export.
- [Media.io AI Micro Drama Creator](https://www.media.io/video-effects/ai-micro-drama-creator.html) — Online tool that turns a micro-drama script, hook or cliffhanger into styled 9:16 short drama clips.
- [Novi AI (AI Short Drama Generator)](https://www.noviai.ai/ai-short-drama-generator/) — Video agent that turns a story idea, script or novel into short drama videos with locked characters, dialogue, narration and subtitles.
- [OpenArt AI Drama Generator](https://openart.ai/features/ai-drama-generator/) — Feature of the OpenArt suite that turns a logline, script or novel chapter into vertical episodic micro-dramas with a locked cast.
- [OpenMontage Drama Studio](https://github.com/charliewwdev/OpenMontage) — Self-hosted AGPL-3.0 web studio that takes a one-line idea through seven stages (script, characters, scenes, props, storyboard and shots, voice, edit), with Seedance shot video using the character, scene and prop images as references.
- [Pexo (AI Micro Drama Maker)](https://pexo.ai/create/micro-drama) — AI video agent that turns a plot line or product photo into hook-first vertical micro-drama episodes with narration, dubbing and identity lock.
- [Pippit (Short Drama / AI Drama Generator)](https://www.pippit.ai/create/short-dramas) — Marketing-video suite whose AI drama generator and Short Drama Agent turn a script or idea into styled, episode-organised short drama clips.
- [PopShort AI (Drama Canvas)](https://popshort.ai/en) — Short-drama production studio where a story idea, script or novel is broken into reusable characters, scenes and props and produced as episodes.
- [SceneMixer](https://scenemixer.com/) — Script or novel in, multi-episode series out: the text is parsed into cast, locations, props and episodes, every character gets a reference sheet, every episode a storyboard, every segment a video clip (Wan 3.0 by default, other tiers selectable), and the clips are assembled into downloadable episodes. Dialogue is spoken in 17 language options; 16:9 by default with 9:16 selectable. 23 public sample series and per-step pricing are on the site.
- [Showrunner (Fable Studio)](https://www.showrunnerstudio.com/) — Serialized AI video app where users build a show world with persistent characters and generate, watch and remix micro-drama episodes.
- [StoReel Canvas](https://canvas.storeel.com/en/) — Short-drama creation workspace from the StoReel microdrama platform covering scriptwriting, storyboarding, voiceover and video generation.
- [StoryShort](https://storyshort.ai/tools/ai-short-drama-generator) — Faceless and story video generator with a short drama tool that makes 30-second to 3-minute vertical episodes with on-screen lip-synced dialogue.
- [Toonkit](https://toonkit.io/en/ai-anime-generator) — AI animation platform that turns web novels, webtoon synopses and scripts into anime with persistent characters for weekly series.
- [Topview Drama Studio](https://www.topview.ai/drama-studio) — Agent-led drama planner and workbench that expands an idea, script or novel chapter into characters, episodes, storyboards and AI video scenes.
- [TrendShort](https://trendshort.com/en) — Script-to-short-drama tool that turns a script or outline into storyboards, character visuals, voiceover captions and a 9:16 drama cut.
- [Wondershare Filmora (AI Short Drama)](https://filmora.wondershare.com/templates/ai-short-drama.html) — Desktop/mobile video editor with an AI short drama workflow: AI screenplay drafting, Seedance 2.0 generation and timeline editing to 4K export.

## Story and faceless video

Story-first generators that produce narrated or captioned videos rather than acted episodes.

- [AutoFaceless.ai](https://autofaceless.ai/) — Faceless channel automation that creates and posts topic-based video series daily; landing page directs to a waitlist.
- [Crreo AI](https://www.crreo.ai/) — Script-to-video maker for 1 to 30-minute narrated videos with recurring characters, voice control and music, marketed for micro-dramas among other formats.
- [Elser AI](https://www.elser.ai/anime) — AI anime and movie generator that turns a story idea or script into styled animated shorts, drama episodes and trailers with voiceover.
- [Faceless.so](https://faceless.so/) — Faceless video automation that writes, voices, edits and auto-posts niche story and fact videos as scheduled series.
- [MagicLight](https://magiclight.ai/) — Story-to-video platform that turns a story or script into a long-form animated or realistic video with recurring characters, voiceover and lip sync.
- [Mootion](https://www.mootion.com/) — AI storytelling video generator that turns prompts, scripts, audio or links into multi-scene narrative videos with consistent characters, voiceover and 3D camera control.
- [OiiOii](https://www.oiioii.ai/) — Multi-agent AI animation platform where seven role agents write, design, animate, edit and score animated shorts and episodic shorts from a prompt.
- [Revid AI](https://www.revid.ai/) — Text/URL-to-video generator for short-form and multi-minute faceless videos, including manga-style story videos and an animated series showcase.
- [Story.com](https://www.story.com/) — AI movie maker and video editor with a movie agent that turns a prompt or script into short films, storybooks and storyboards.
- [SwipeStory](https://www.swipestory.click/) — Faceless story video generator that produces and auto-publishes niche story series for TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.
- [TaleTok](https://taletok.io/) — Faceless YouTube automation service that generates and posts narrated story videos (e.g. Reddit stories) daily.

- [YouTube — disclosing altered or synthetic content](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491) — What creators must label on YouTube and how the label appears.
- [TikTok — AI-generated content](https://www.tiktok.com/transparency/en/ai-generated-content/) — TikTok's labelling rules and automatic detection for AI-generated content.
- [C2PA — Content Credentials](https://c2pa.org/) — The open standard for provenance metadata that several generators write into files.
- [Wikipedia — Text-to-video model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Text-to-video_model) — Background article with a history of the field and its models.
